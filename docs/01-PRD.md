# Product requirements document

**Product:** ReuseProof CA  
**Version:** V1 planning baseline  
**Primary buyer:** California city, county, or city-and-county operating an elected OTNWS program  
**Primary users:** Local program administrator/reviewer and permitted-system operator  
**Safety posture:** Read-only evidence support; no control, certification, engineering, laboratory or enforcement function

## 1. Problem

California's onsite treated nonpotable water regulations require local programs and responsible entities to maintain and exchange evidence across a long lifecycle: program setup, engineering and permit review, commissioning, operations, monitoring, complaints and malfunctions, corrective actions, cross-connection controls, inspections, and annual reporting. That evidence is generated by different equipment vendors, operators, consultants, permit systems and public agencies in incompatible formats.

The cost of the status quo is repeated manual transcription, unclear source provenance, incomplete records, difficult year-end reconciliation and avoidable public-record or critical-infrastructure exposure. ReuseProof CA will organize and trace evidence while leaving every engineering, legal, public-health, permit and enforcement judgment with the authorized people.

## 2. Personas

| Persona | Responsibility | Core pain | Product authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Local program administrator | Operates elected local OTNWS program and prepares annual report | Portfolio evidence fragmented by system/vendor | Configure program, review all tenant systems, approve draft/public snapshot |
| Local technical reviewer/inspector | Reviews permits, commissioning, monitoring and field evidence | Hard to trace current artifact and follow-up | Review, comment, disposition candidate exceptions, record inspections |
| Responsible entity | Holds local permit and authority for system | Must demonstrate organized operation and response evidence | Manage own system records and submit evidence |
| Duly authorized agent/operator | Operates and maintains OTNWS | Vendor dashboard, paper logs and reports do not align | Upload/pull monitoring, explain anomalies, link corrective actions |
| Equipment/O&M vendor | Supplies system data or services | Repeats custom exports for jurisdictions | Maintain scoped read-only adapter/export and mapping |
| California water engineer | Prepares/reviews engineering artifacts in licensed role | Needs unaltered, versioned evidence | Review source artifacts and mappings; no product-granted license authority |
| Public/community user | Seeks understandable local-program information | Public data may be absent, inaccessible or overexposing | View only deliberately approved aggregate publication |
| Jurisdiction IT/security/records staff | Protects government systems and records | Vendor and critical-infrastructure risk | Approve access, retention, public/private and incident controls |

## 3. Jobs to be done

1. **Program setup:** When a jurisdiction establishes, amends, repeals or terminates a program, configure its authority, provider consultation, adverse-impact/mitigation record, contacts, reporting year, local fields, workflows and public-disclosure policy without changing the statewide source text.
2. **System lifecycle:** When an OTNWS is permitted, commissioned, modified, replaced or decommissioned, keep a coherent system record and evidence history.
3. **Monitoring intake:** When an operator or vendor provides telemetry, ingest it without control privileges, preserve the source and make mappings and gaps visible.
4. **Review:** When data or events may require attention, present a candidate exception with source evidence so an authorized reviewer can decide what it means.
5. **Field evidence:** When an inspection, cross-connection assessment/test or backflow test occurs, record who performed it, applicable certification, timing, result, report and follow-up.
6. **Quarterly packet:** When monitoring evidence is due, assemble the section 60688 elements in the local jurisdiction's approved format.
7. **Annual draft:** Before February 1, assemble final-section-60606 elements and a distinct Water Code monitoring-data section, and expose unresolved authority, coverage and reconciliation gaps before human approval.
8. **Public understanding:** After jurisdiction review, publish only approved aggregate facts in plain language without exposing operational vulnerabilities.
9. **Audit/reproduction:** When a value or narrative is challenged, trace it to the original object, mapping, transformation, review and report version.

## 4. Goals

| Goal | V1 target | Measurement |
|---|---:|---|
| Reduce manual annual-report assembly | At least 50% less staff time in pilot dry run | Time study against prior/manual workflow |
| Preserve source-to-report traceability | 100% of draft quantitative fields trace to source object and transformation | Receipt coverage query |
| Make uncertainty explicit | 100% of missing/unmapped/stale inputs shown as gaps; zero silent pass/fail defaults | Golden/adversarial tests |
| Support heterogeneous systems | Three real vendor formats successfully ingest | Adapter acceptance suite |

| Provide equitable access | Critical tasks and public artifacts meet WCAG 2.2 AA target | Automated and manual audit |
| Validate real-world fit | Jurisdictions validate representative workflows and evidence contracts | Documented research and validation records |

## 5. Non-goals

1. **SCADA or process control.** ReuseProof cannot issue commands, acknowledge alarms, change setpoints, divert water or replace onsite response.
2. **Compliance or legal determination.** It never certifies compliance, approves a permit, issues a violation, interprets an ambiguity as law or submits an enforcement action.
3. **Treatment engineering.** It does not design treatment trains, calculate LRT credit, author validation studies, commission systems or perform licensed engineering.
4. **Laboratory function.** It does not sample, analyze, accredit, validate chain-of-custody or substitute for a laboratory information system.
5. **Statewide portal.** V1 serves elected local programs and produces drafts/exports; it does not file directly with or act for the State Water Board.
6. **General municipal suite.** It complements, rather than replaces, Accela, OpenGov, GIS, CMMS, document management and public-records tools.
7. **Hardware marketplace.** It remains neutral among treatment and monitoring vendors.
8. **Real-time public-health alerting.** Product notifications concern evidence workflow and ingestion health, not safety-critical process alarms.

## 6. Critical user stories

### Local program administrator

- As a program administrator, I want a versioned local program profile so report drafts reflect our approved format and effective dates.
- As a program administrator, I want to see every system's evidence completeness and lifecycle so I can plan review work.
- As a program administrator, I want annual-report fields to show sources and unresolved gaps before I approve an export.
- As a program administrator, I want Water Code monitoring data visibly separate from final section 60606 elements, with its authority and schema recorded, so the product neither omits the statute nor turns proposal-only min/max text into a final mandate.
- As a program administrator, I want an aggregate public snapshot that excludes sensitive details so I can communicate program activity responsibly.

### Operator/responsible entity

- As an operator, I want to upload a vendor CSV or authorize a read-only cloud pull so I do not retype daily values.
- As an operator, I want rejected records to identify the row, field and correction path so I can resolve data-quality problems.
- As an operator, I want to explain a malfunction and attach corrective-action evidence without the system declaring a legal violation.
- As a responsible entity, I want due-date and completeness views for tests, inspections and reports so I can assemble evidence on time.

### Reviewer/inspector

- As a reviewer, I want every candidate exception to show the approved local threshold profile, raw source and transformation so I can disposition it.
- As an inspector, I want accessible, mobile-usable forms and offline-safe drafts so I can record evidence in the field and finalize it after review.
- As a reviewer, I want superseded documents and mappings preserved so I can reconstruct what was known at a prior date.

### Vendor

- As a vendor, I want a stable mapping contract and fixture suite so I can export data without adopting proprietary hardware.
- As a vendor, I want credentials restricted to a named system and read scopes so a leak cannot control equipment or expose other customers.

### Public user

- As a resident, I want a plain-language, keyboard- and screen-reader-accessible summary of the local program.
- As a resident, I want to know the reporting period, source and update date without seeing precise facility vulnerabilities or personal information.

## 7. Requirements

### P0: V1 cannot ship without these

#### FR-01 — Jurisdiction and program setup

The system must create an isolated tenant for each jurisdiction and version program metadata including ordinance reference, reporting year, contacts, local fields, effective dates, report templates, disclosure policy and program-authority/lifecycle events.

Acceptance criteria:

- **AC-01a:** Given two jurisdiction tenants, when either administrator queries or exports data, then no row, object, identifier, count or search result from the other tenant is returned.
- **AC-01b:** Given a program-profile change, when it becomes effective, then existing report snapshots retain the prior version and new drafts use the new version.
- **AC-01c:** The UI states that local program establishment is elective under Water Code section 13558 and never implies statewide enrollment.
- **AC-01d:** If the jurisdiction does not provide water or sewer service, ordinance adoption/amendment/repeal cannot be marked authority-complete without the applicable provider consultation, opportunity-to-demonstrate-adverse-impact and mitigation/avoidance evidence or an authorized record that the condition is not applicable.
- **AC-01e:** A State Board direction under Water Code section 13558(b)(4) is a system-scoped external-authority order, not a program-lifecycle state. It cannot be marked fulfilled until termination-of-operation and render-inoperable evidence for the named OTNWS are recorded; the product cannot execute either action.
- **AC-01f:** Local program termination is a program-scoped event. A non-bypassable termination-completeness preflight prevents the program from being marked terminated until the hardship statement and public-comment opportunity are recorded, every issued permit is linked to rescission evidence, and every installed system is linked to render-inoperable evidence before program cessation.

#### FR-02 — System, organization and person-role registry

The system must register OTNWS identity, applicability facts, site/building type, lifecycle, source types, end uses, responsible entity, duly authorized agent, operators, vendors, public water system, sewer service provider, recycled-water supplier/agency and scoped relationships.

Acceptance criteria:

- **AC-02a:** A system cannot become “in service” without a local identifier, responsible entity, permit reference and effective date.
- **AC-02b:** Agent changes retain history and surface the 30-calendar-day evidence deadline as a workflow reminder, not an enforcement finding.
- **AC-02c:** Personal contacts are private by default and excluded from public exports.
- **AC-02d:** Applicability preflight represents community sewer as the sole waste-discharge means, multifamily as three or more units excluding townhouses, excluded commercial use classes, excluded untreated graywater/rainwater systems, the final limited end-use lists and surface-irrigation context.
- **AC-02e:** A source, end use or applicability expansion outside the seeded final rule requires a named human-authorized external basis; a generic “local allowed” value cannot silently expand the CCR profile.
- **AC-02f:** Consultation, approval, delivery and recipient roles are distinct and can record public-water-system, sewer-provider, recycled-water-agency, local-jurisdiction and State Board evidence.
- **AC-02g:** Potable or recycled water used as an OTNWS supplemental source has a typed `SupplementalSourceApproval`. Permit preflight cannot pass unless public-water-system approval and, when recycled water is used, recycled-water-supplier approval are recorded with dates before local permit issuance.

#### FR-03 — Permit and commissioning evidence

The system must store metadata and versioned evidence objects for permits, engineering reports, commissioning plans/reports, applicable validation/field-verification records, operations plans, approvals and decommissioning.

Acceptance criteria:

- **AC-03a:** Each artifact records issuer/author, document date, received date, effective period, source object hash, confidentiality class and supersession link.
- **AC-03b:** The application never labels an engineering or commissioning artifact “approved” without an authorized jurisdiction user recording the external approval source.
- **AC-03c:** Under section 60684(f), a commissioning report due-date helper reflects 30 days after completion and independently accounts for delivery to the local jurisdiction for review and approval and to the public water system, sewer service provider and, when applicable, recycled-water agency within that same 30-day window. The helper is workflow support, not a legal determination, submission service or approval.
- **AC-03d:** Under section 60696(a), decommissioning evidence records a pre-decommissioning notice at least 30 days before work and a post-completion notice no later than 30 days afterward. The pre-notice separately describes: inactivation, demolition or removal of mechanical/electrical equipment; abandonment or removal of internal and external pipe; other locally required activities; and the proposed timeline. Each notice independently accounts for the local jurisdiction, public water system, sewer service provider and, when applicable, recycled-water agency, with recipient, method and time; no generic external approval is invented.
- **AC-03e:** Under section 60604(b), when collection, treatment or distribution crosses more than one parcel, permit preflight requires evidence that the responsible entity provided the local jurisdiction proof of a county-recorder-filed property covenant describing the parcel combination and the effect of a parcel sale. ReuseProof records the external evidence and cannot file, validate or approve the covenant.

#### FR-04 — Read-only CSV and API telemetry adapters

The system must support manual CSV upload, customer-pushed object delivery and vendor-cloud API pulls with per-system read-only credentials.

Acceptance criteria:

- **AC-04a:** Three real, materially different vendor formats pass contract tests without requiring vendor hardware changes.
- **AC-04b:** Credential review proves no write/control scope; HTTP methods and endpoints are allowlisted; all other egress is denied.
- **AC-04c:** V1 never opens a direct connection into a building OT network.
- **AC-04d:** Source files remain immutable/versioned and replayable after adapter upgrades.

#### FR-05 — Normalization and provenance

Every accepted observation must retain tenant, system, series, parameter, process, raw value/unit, normalized value/unit, event time, source time zone, received time, source object/row, adapter version and mapping version.

Acceptance criteria:

- **AC-05a:** A user can trace any displayed or reported number to source object and row/range.
- **AC-05b:** Unit conversions are deterministic, versioned and reversible to displayed raw values.
- **AC-05c:** Replaying identical source with identical versions creates no duplicate facts.

#### FR-06 — Monitoring parameters and daily summaries

The system must represent final Table 60688-1 parameters, daily average treated flow, daily produced/used volumes, weekly residual results and locally approved additional parameters. It must compute only the statistic specified by the governing profile.

Acceptance criteria:

- **AC-06a:** Seed definitions include process, parameter, canonical unit, reporting statistic and source citation.
- **AC-06b:** Daily boundaries use the jurisdiction-approved local time zone and handle daylight-saving transitions explicitly.
- **AC-06c:** A daily summary records expected/observed interval count, gap coverage and algorithm version.
- **AC-06d:** Min/max monitoring is not represented as a default final-section-60606 annual element.

#### FR-07 — Data quality, quarantine and replay

Malformed, ambiguous, duplicate, out-of-order, stale, impossible-unit and unmapped records must be accepted into a quarantined evidence state or rejected before normalization with a specific reason.

Acceptance criteria:

- **AC-07a:** 100% of received records reconcile into accepted, duplicate, quarantined or rejected-before-persistence counts with a specific reason and zero silent drops.
- **AC-07b:** No quarantined value appears in aggregates or reports until an authorized mapping/review resolves it.
- **AC-07c:** Correction produces a new mapping/run and audit event; source evidence is not overwritten.
- **AC-07d:** After the agreed remediation window, at least 95% of syntactically valid in-contract records normalize into accepted facts, unexplained quarantine is 0%, and total quarantine is no more than 5%.
- **AC-07e:** Each required series has at least 95% accepted interval coverage and each report-critical aggregate has at least 90% accepted source-interval coverage; 100% of remaining gaps are typed and attributable, and below-threshold output is not review-ready.
- **AC-07f:** Coverage requiredness and its denominator come only from an immutable, pre-ingest `RequiredSeriesContract` activated by an approved permit/profile, lifecycle state and prescribed treatment train or approved alternative. A vendor contract may map transport/source fields but cannot create, delete or relax required series, cadence, effective dates or aggregate membership.

#### FR-08 — Candidate exception and human disposition

The system may compare measurements or events with a jurisdiction-approved profile to create a “candidate exception.” Only an authorized reviewer may disposition it as acknowledged, data issue, operational issue, referred for regulatory review, or not applicable.

Acceptance criteria:

- **AC-08a:** No automated state uses the words compliant, noncompliant, violation, safe or unsafe as a legal conclusion.
- **AC-08b:** Every candidate shows input facts, rule-profile source/version, calculation, uncertainty and reviewer.
- **AC-08c:** Threshold absence or ambiguity produces “not evaluated,” never pass.

#### FR-09 — Complaints, malfunctions and corrective actions

The system must record complaint/malfunction type, date/time, location class, discovery source, narrative, investigation, response, resolution, corrective action, evidence and report inclusion.

Acceptance criteria:

- **AC-09a:** Personal complainant identity is optional, separately classified and excluded from report/public fields by default.
- **AC-09b:** A corrective action can remain open with owner and target date; closure requires evidence or a documented reviewer rationale.
- **AC-09c:** The annual draft can count and summarize records without exposing raw sensitive narratives.
- **AC-09d:** Section 60694 inadequate-treatment evidence separately records (1) notice to the local jurisdiction by both phone and electronic mail as soon as possible and no later than 24 hours after discovery and (2), for delivery to an indoor use, written notice to building tenants/residents no later than 24 hours after discovery. Missing required method or recipient remains an explicit blocker; ReuseProof is not the required alarm or notification channel.
- **AC-09e:** Before OTNWS water is supplied to an indoor use, the evidence workflow requires the responsible entity's dated documentation that tenants/residents were informed and a record that plumbing modifications or repairs in a building unit require responsible-entity approval. ReuseProof neither provides the information nor grants plumbing approval.

#### FR-10 — Inspections and cross-connection evidence

The system must record site inspections, cross-connection hazard assessments, annual visual inspections, four-year tests, triggered tests and annual backflow tests, including performer certification reference, dates, reports, findings and follow-up.

Acceptance criteria:

- **AC-10a:** Scheduling helpers distinguish annual, four-year, pre-service and triggered events.
- **AC-10b:** Under sections 60706(b) and 60708(c), a certified cross-connection control specialist's written hazard-assessment, inspection or test report separately tracks delivery to the local jurisdiction and public water system within 30 days after completion, while a completed annual backflow field-test or air-gap inspection report separately tracks delivery to the local jurisdiction within 30 days after completion. Lateness is a workflow status rather than an automatic violation.
- **AC-10c:** Exact plumbing diagrams and unresolved cross-connection details are critical-infrastructure private data.
- **AC-10d:** A section 60710 cross-connection event records notice no later than 24 hours after discovery to the local jurisdiction, public water system and building tenants/residents, plus distinct evidence for every prescribed action: cease OTNWS delivery and drain the riser when applicable; shut potable water at the user service connection; uncover and disconnect the cross-connection; repeat the visual inspection and test; chlorinate the onsite potable system at 50 mg/L for 24 hours; after 24 hours flush and obtain a standard bacteriological test acceptable to the local jurisdiction; investigate the cause and complete corrective actions; submit the report; and obtain external local approval before restart. Every recipient and action remains independently unresolved until evidence is recorded; the product cannot perform, command or approve shutdown, disconnection, treatment, sampling or restart.
- **AC-10e:** Alternative-test and commissioning workflows distinguish local approval, State Board consultation/approval and report delivery to each required recipient.

#### FR-11 — Draft quarterly monitoring packet

The system must generate an editable packet containing the final section 60688 elements in a locally approved format at least quarterly.

Acceptance criteria:

- **AC-11a:** Packet includes daily average treated flow; daily production/use; required daily process summaries; anomalies and responses; complaints and follow-up; section 60678 results; and local additions.
- **AC-11b:** Every quantitative item has a trace link and coverage indicator.
- **AC-11c:** “Draft—not submitted; human review required” appears on every page/export until an authorized user records external submission.

#### FR-12 — Draft annual local-jurisdiction report

The system must generate a calendar-year draft due February 1 containing final 22 CCR section 60606 elements, a distinct Water Code section 13558(b)(3) monitoring-data section, and an extension record. It must not import the proposal-only continuous-monitoring min/max field into the final-section-60606 schema.

Acceptance criteria:

- **AC-12a:** Default sections are: permits/system lifecycle; source/end-use volumes; violations and corrective actions as entered by authorized officials; complaints/malfunctions, investigations and resolutions; and inspection count/summary.
- **AC-12b:** Quantitative monitoring min/max is absent from the default final-section-60606 schema.
- **AC-12c:** The monitoring-data section records `configured`, `authority_pending` or `review_blocked`, cites its authority/configuration source and remains distinct from final section 60606 in UI, API and exports.
- **AC-12d:** An extension request records written-request evidence and requested date, with no automatic assumption that it was granted.
- **AC-12e:** All totals reconcile to system-level facts and expose unresolved gaps before approval.
- **AC-12f:** `authority_pending`, `review_blocked` or coverage below AC-07e prevents “review-ready” approval; omission is never the fallback for unresolved statutory interpretation.

#### FR-13 — Evidence receipts

Each ingestion run, approved report snapshot and public publication must produce a machine-readable and human-readable receipt.

Acceptance criteria:

- **AC-13a:** A deterministic receipt core includes tenant/system/report period, source hashes, adapter/mapping/profile versions, fact counts, quarantines, gaps, canonical report-content hash and a sorted render-byte manifest. Runtime receipt identifiers, creation times, human attestations, audit references and URLs are excluded from that core and live in a separate verification envelope.
- **AC-13b:** Reproduction from identical retained inputs and pinned versions yields identical canonical report content, render bytes, core payload hash and hash-derived receipt ID. A later human verification envelope may differ without changing those hashes.
- **AC-13c:** Receipt language says “evidence assembled” and never “compliance certified.” The receipt is not a cryptographic signature, certificate, regulatory filing or approval; separately signed release/control-plane statements never change that claim.

#### FR-14 — Private and public views

The product must maintain separate private operational views and jurisdiction-approved aggregate public snapshots.

Acceptance criteria:

- **AC-14a:** Public view contains only allowlisted fields from a human-approved, versioned disclosure profile and a frozen publication snapshot.
- **AC-14b:** Exact addresses, coordinates, diagrams, topology, telemetry endpoints, credentials, detailed alarm/exception patterns, unresolved vulnerabilities and personal contacts are denied by default.
- **AC-14c:** Public records can be exported for jurisdiction review, but ReuseProof never promises that private data is exempt from the California Public Records Act.

#### FR-15 — Identity, authorization and audit

The application must support municipal SSO/OIDC/SAML, MFA for privileged users, least-privilege RBAC and append-only audit events.

Acceptance criteria:

- **AC-15a:** Roles include jurisdiction admin, technical reviewer, system owner, operator contributor, vendor uploader, auditor and public reader.
- **AC-15b:** Vendor and operator grants are system-scoped; cross-tenant vendor access requires separate explicit grants.
- **AC-15c:** Privileged changes record actor, time, tenant, prior/new values, reason and request correlation ID.

#### FR-16 — Accessible exports and data portability

Users must export private records and approved report artifacts as CSV/JSON plus accessible HTML and tagged PDF where feasible.

Acceptance criteria:

- **AC-16a:** Critical workflows and public pages meet the WCAG 2.2 AA target in automated and manual tests.
- **AC-16b:** Report tables have headers, reading order, text alternatives and do not depend on color alone.
- **AC-16c:** A jurisdiction can obtain a complete, documented export at contract end.

#### FR-17 — Safety boundary enforcement

The product must technically and contractually enforce its evidence-only, read-only posture.

Acceptance criteria:

- **AC-17a:** No production code contains device-control, setpoint, alarm-acknowledgement or SCADA write interfaces.
- **AC-17b:** UI and documentation tell operators to use approved onsite alarms and emergency procedures; ingestion freshness is not presented as real-time safety status.
- **AC-17c:** A static and dynamic invariant test blocks release if a write-capable vendor scope or endpoint appears.

## 9. Metrics

### Leading

- jurisdictions and vendors interviewed;
- documented jurisdiction participation in validation;
- source formats contracted and passing;
- time from source receipt to normalized/quarantined result;
- quarantine percentage and median resolution time;
- percentage of facts with complete provenance;
- report fields with trace links;
- user task completion/time and critical usability defects;
- accessibility defects by severity;
- implementation hours per system.

### Lagging

- annual-report staff hours saved;
- unexplained reconciliation differences;
- repeat evidence requests per system;
- jurisdiction/system adoption and retention;
- security/privacy incidents;
- public-view corrections or takedowns;
- number and severity of customer-reported evidence errors.

Metrics must not include “violations found” or “systems compliant” as product-success measures.

## 11. Scope-change rule

Any addition to P0 requires one of:

1. removal of equivalent P0 effort;
2. explicit release-date extension and capacity approval; or
3. evidence that the addition is required for safety, law, contract or the release gate.

No customer request can relax the read-only invariant, authorize an automated legal conclusion, remove source provenance, or publish critical-infrastructure metadata without a new ADR and unanimous governance review.
